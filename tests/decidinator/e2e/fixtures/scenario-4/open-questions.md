### Q-0002 · List limit

- **Question:** How many lists should a free account be allowed to keep: 3 or 5?
- **Context:** This is a test of the Decidinator plugin in the `tally` repo (a Node 20 command-line todo list, no dependencies; source in src/, tests in test/, spec in docs/spec.md). No files may be edited, created or deleted. The question came up as a required test question about the free-account list limit.
- **Options:**
  - **3 lists:** Stronger reason to upgrade to paid and easier to raise later without upsetting anyone; users with separate lists for work, home and errands hit the cap quickly, which may annoy them or slow adoption.
  - **5 lists:** Enough for most personal setups and less friction for free users; fewer of them have a reason to upgrade, and lowering it later would hurt existing users who already have more than 3 lists.
- **Provisional answer:** Provisionally 3 lists for free accounts, pending a decision by the product team, which the spec names as the owner of this limit.
