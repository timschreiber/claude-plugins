## Blocking

- `plugins/orcastrat/skills/run/SKILL.md:278` — When the combined re-verify (item 9) fails, `run` writes the blocks and goes to Stop, but skips item 10. The other integrated tasks' commits are already on the plan branch, yet those tasks get no status commit and no `Orcastrat-Task:` trailer. Their Status stays `todo`, their reports and failure logs stay in their worktrees, and the worktrees aren't removed. Before M07, the integrated commits carried the trailer themselves, so `recover` found them as `done`. Now, on the next run, 2a item 4 stops on the leftover worktrees. Once the user removes them, `recover` reports those tasks as `interrupted`, which ends in a SETUP stop or a reset. So after a VERIFY stop in a parallel wave, the run can't resume from git (M07-T08; D120, D57; spec §25a; Coverage §6 item 6)
